## Description (BBCode)

[h1]The problem[/h1]

Research schematics are books that grant progress toward a research project. Vanilla decides
which projects can have one by the DLC feature they belong to rather than by whether you can
research them, so the Biotech mechtech line, the Odyssey gravtech line, and every Royalty
project needing a techprint are excluded outright.

It keeps refusing after you unlock them. Recruit a mechanitor, inspect a grav engine, apply
the techprint — the research opens up at the bench, but no book about it will ever exist.

The longer a colony runs, the more this bites. Once you have researched everything else,
every schematic you find is for something you already know.

[h1]What this mod does[/h1]

Schematics generate for a project once your colony has actually unlocked it. Meet the
requirement and books on that subject start appearing like any other.

Do not meet it and nothing changes. A colony that never takes a mechanitor never sees a
mechtech schematic, so your book pool does not fill up with research you cannot use. If a
requirement later lapses, generation stops again — the same way the research itself re-locks.

Books you already own are left alone. They stay readable, exactly as in vanilla.

Works with modded research too, including techprint-gated projects added by other mods.

[h1]Notes[/h1]

[list]
[*] Requires Harmony.
[*] RimWorld 1.6.
[*] Safe to add to an existing save.
[/list]
