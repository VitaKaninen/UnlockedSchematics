## Description (BBCode)

In vanilla, schematics only spawn for research that is not locked behind a techprint, a
mechanitor, or a grav engine. Those topics never produce a book — not before you unlock
them, and not after.

That matters if research is not how your colony advances — forbidden by your ideoligion,
slowed to a crawl by your scenario, or simply not how you want to play. When schematics are
your route to new technology, a large part of the game's content is permanently out of reach.

This mod fixes that. Schematics spawn for those topics as soon as your colony meets the
requirement — the techprint is applied, a mechanitor joins, a grav engine is inspected.

Nothing else changes. Requirements you have not met still produce no books, so your pool
never fills up with research you cannot use, and books you already own are untouched.

Works with modded research too, including techprint-gated projects added by other mods.

It also drops the research bench from the two interactions that only ever used it as a
place to stand. Applying a techprint and analysing a mech chip normally send a pawn to a
bench, so a colony that never researches still has to build one and keep it powered for
those alone. With this mod, if there is no bench your pawn can use, both happen where the
item lies. If you do have a usable bench, nothing changes - the pawn carries the item
there exactly as before. The mechanitor requirement on chips is untouched.

[list]
[*] Requires Harmony.
[*] RimWorld 1.6.
[*] Safe to add to an existing save.
[/list]
