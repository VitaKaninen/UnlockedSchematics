## Description (BBCode)

In vanilla, schematics only spawn for research that is not locked behind a techprint, a
mechanitor, or a grav engine. Those topics never produce a book — not before you unlock
them, and not after.

That is a real problem if you want to run a research-less colony and unlock technology
through schematics instead: a large portion of the game's content is permanently out of
reach.

This mod fixes that. Schematics spawn for those topics as soon as your colony meets the
requirement — the techprint is applied, a mechanitor joins, a grav engine is inspected.

Nothing else changes. Requirements you have not met still produce no books, so your pool
never fills up with research you cannot use, and books you already own are untouched.

Works with modded research too, including techprint-gated projects added by other mods.

[list]
[*] Requires Harmony.
[*] RimWorld 1.6.
[*] Safe to add to an existing save.
[/list]
